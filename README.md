# Privacy Policy - Anchor

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy describes how Anchor ("we", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
Anchor is designed to work entirely on your device. All data related to archery match scores, settings, and user preferences is stored **exclusively locally on your device**.

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or match scores to external servers or cloud storage.

## 2. Third-Party Services
The application uses standard libraries (such as audio plugins and system preferences) that operate locally without tracking user activities for advertising or profiling purposes.

## 3. Security
Since your data remains on your device, its security is protected by your phone's native security systems (passcode, Face ID / Touch ID).

## 4. Contact
For any questions or concerns regarding this policy, you can contact us through the app's support channels.
